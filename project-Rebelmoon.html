Rebel Moon: Blood Line

I had the pleasure of working as an Environment Artist on Zack Snyder's Rebel Moon tie-in game Blood Line at SEMC!

As always, all the work you see here is a gigantic team effort, thank you so much for the team at SEMC for the opportunity to work on this, and our amazing partners at Netflix.
My responsibilities were mostly set dressing, navmesh, collision and asset implementation towards the final 3 months of the project, and at the preprod stage I did some feedback rounds with the team.

Cheers!
